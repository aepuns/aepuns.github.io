about aepuns

Hey everyone! Aepuns here. I deliberated on this for a while, and eventually decided the best way to do this would be to treat it as more of an autobiography. Makes it feel more like you're hearing this straight from the horse's mouth and not from the chat's gpt :)

So, to begin, we should probably jump to about three years before I created the channel, 2017. I was a strange kid, always seeing things as more "official" or "professional" than they should be. Requiring myself to think about turning everyone one of my ideas into a franchise or TV series. I quite literally designed a LOGO for my FRIEND GROUP at the time, named "The Square with One Dot." Long story.

Anyway, I even planned to put the logo on hats for the five of us. That was probably the most ambitious dream at the time. That, and a wildly successful Youtube channel, of course. We would always daydream about having our own channels, inspired by the big names of the time, the likes of which you've probably heard of before. Some niche-er picks for me, though, were probably InTheLittleWood and Sqaishey. (Especially Sqaishey's Cake Quest series. I cried when that ended. 100% genuinely.)

Of course, I won't out my friends with their... time appropriate... channel name ideas, but mine is unfortunately pretty relevant. "Sparkling Sundae"

Some of you may already know the importance behind me telling you this, but we'll get to that later.

Anyway, skip ahead a little to (probably late) March of 2020, I don't know, I don't have exact dates. I was watching the likes of Nexus and Viprin and thinking, "Y'know, these guys really only showcase, like, levels from big creators and levels that are currently kinda viral... hm. Somebody should really cover some lesser known levels that they personally like..." And so I decided to do that.

My Youtube channel was still called SparklingSundae at the time, before later being shorted to GD Sundae, which I eventually ended up changing my entire online identity to. (Including basically just Discord and Newgrounds at the time.) THIS is where things start picking up. I would end up going by GD Sundae for close to a year, before I began to feel that the name was getting outdated. Nobody uses "GD" before their name anymore. Plus people were using the alias as a reason to pretend to vore me and unfortunately for those people I'm not into that.

So one night, December 21st, 2020, I started brainstorming how I could change my name. In fact, I was sitting in the same spot I am right now. While I was writing, I had to erase something from the page. Whatever it was, I can't remember, but it's not like it was important, I was erasing it. I flipped my pencil back around to start writing again, and brushed that gross eraser residue or whatever off the page. (You know what I'm talking about.) Though I wasn't careful enough to hold the sheet down with my other hand, knocking it off the drawing table I was using, as well as off my bed. I had to set my pencil down, and I did so quite quickly, looking over at a more clear spot on my bed to set it down without it rolling away, and sat up to grab the page that had fallen off. For whatever reason, and in the last stroke of luck I had saved up for the rest of my life, my eyes landed directly on the word "Sundae" when I whipped my head around, and before I could pick the paper up, my brain registered it as: "aepunS? When did I write that?" for only a split second, before it locked in and recognized that, with my handwriting at the time, "Sundae" looked strikingly like "aepunS" when rotated 180 degrees. (And in most fonts it still does.)

Even then, I didn't quite recognize it as an alias option until I was called up for supper, and I began to think when I was at the table. No way anybody had ever used "aepuns" before, right? And no way it was a previously established term. In ANY language. There was just no way. Soon enough, I had made "æpuns" my Discord username. More as a joke than anything, as I changed my username a lot back then, but soon enough I started to like it, I just didn't like how I had merged the A and E to make it look like some foreign word. So I ended up deciding sometime between January 1st and January 10th of 2021, that, because it was so unique, and, more importantly, special to me, that I would start going by "aepuns."

I would change my channel name to that as well, as I fully abandoned the "Sundae" pseudonym, finally distancing myself from ever being confused for Ssundee :)
